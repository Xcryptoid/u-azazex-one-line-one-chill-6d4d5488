A single sentence of horror: a smart speaker, a dead father's favourite song, and a reply that turns the request back on the person who asked.

## Entry

> I asked the smart speaker to play my dead father's favourite song, and it said, 'He's been asking for you too.'

111 characters, one line, one sentence of dread.

## How it runs

`main.py` is a stdlib-only copier: it copies `entry.txt` into `MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR` unchanged.
